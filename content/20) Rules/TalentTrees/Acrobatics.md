A proficiency in acrobatics represent deft control over your
body. Whether you are sliding across a sheet of ice, balancing
on a tightrope, or trying to stay upright amid the fury of the
raging sea, your proficiency in acrobatics helps you stay on your
toes. Continuing to improve your skill lets you learn to tumble
your way through danger, evade aimed blows and reckless
volleys, stride through all manner of terrain and accomplish
gravity-defying feats of dexterity.
