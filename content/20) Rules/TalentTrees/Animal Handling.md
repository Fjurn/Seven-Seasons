A proficiency in animal handling represents a special
camaraderie with, control over, and empathy for animals.
Whether you’re raising a puppy, riding a warhorse, or rescuing
an injured eagle, both tame and wild beasts tend to respect,
trust, and listen to you, and you know how to care for them.
Continuing to hone your skill lets you learn to teach animals to
communicate and follow your commands, masterfully control
your mount in the heat of battle, and even stabilize or bolster
them against harm.
