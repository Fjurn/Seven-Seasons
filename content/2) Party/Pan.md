# Portrait
<img width="250" src="https://cdn.artstation.com/p/assets/images/images/007/378/427/large/jinglin-xu-jinglin918xiao.jpg?1505739929"/>

Beneath her robes she wears armor of her own making, each plate shaped, tempered, and fitted by her hands alone. To Pan Xul, the crafting of armor is not mere preparation for battle but a sacred practice — a discipline through which order is forged both in steel and in spirit. The work demands patience, focus, and acceptance of imperfection, and in that labor she finds clarity.

She regards those who entrust their protection to the hands of others with "quiet" disapproval. To wear armor one has not shaped is, to her, to neglect an essential step in learning how to live, endure, and stand with purpose. The forging is meditation, devotion, and instruction all at once. Through heat, hammer, and time, she believes one tempers not only metal, but the self.

Her armor is therefore not simply worn — it is practiced, inhabited, and renewed as a way of life.

# Story
Pan Xul is known in Tenno as an offical within the demon administration. She carries herself with quiet confidence and a sense of purpose, though very few know her true purpose. She often communicates with the demon administration, through letters carried by her faithfull bird, named Yama.

<img width="250" alt="66862b12-6db9-4c61-94d8-96246755e8b5" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/cedf4c8b-ad4b-4150-a5a0-7f13decaf337" />

She claims to be from a distant village in Hanika, one since long destroyed by demons. To the locals, her past is one of survival and loss. Though relatively new to the city, Pan has integrated herself with care. She maintains polite relationships with neighbors and colleagues, attends necessary gatherings, and fulfills her duties with diligence. She lives alone in a modest upper-level apartment, preferring order and simplicity. There is little extravagance in her lifestyle, only quiet discipline.

Those who work beside her may sense that her training runs deeper than standard administration would require. She moves like someone accustomed to structure and hierarchy, and when discussions turn to demon-related matters, her focus sharpens perceptibly.

Pan Xul is, to most, a survivor turned servant of the realm. What lies beyond that is known only to her.!
