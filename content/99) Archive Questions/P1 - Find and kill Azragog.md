Azragog can be found in a plane called the Eternal Sea. He sits at the bottom of the sandy seas of this plane, slumbering in the shadows recovering for his next attack. He can be killed with any weapon, as long as they are magical. Although, at the strength you and your friends are, you might not even leave a scratch on him.
