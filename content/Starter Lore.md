## Rulers, demons and humans
Since the beginning of time, the world has found balance in itself. Humanity lives in a world called [[(7) Cegeris]], which is ruled by demons and their servants. Within the highest ranks of demons, creatures exist that call themselves the Monarchs. They decide, without even lifting a finger, what the fate of the world shall be. From one day to another, they can bring down all forms of life. 

As of lately, the demons from higher planes have been more aggressive during their visits. Their relations with the Rulers have been shattered by an unknown reason and they seek strengths to destroy them. They intend to take their place, become the new rulers. This would most likely mean the end of the human race, as the demons regularly destroy human civilizations just for fun. Where they are now somewhat punished for doing so by the Rulers, they would freely roam the human realm when there are no consequences. 

Humans have been cultivating their powers in order to stand a chance against the demons. Their strengths have changed from simple martial arts to elemental and magical based combat fighting, mostly martial arts and elements combined. Temples, monasteries, and schools have popped up across the world solely to train new generations in these arts. 

Humans and demons have always crossed each other's borders to explore and conquer one another. But these visits have been happening more and more in the past 100 years. Each year more villages get demolished from demonic attacks and more demons are captured from the demon realm. They get exploited out of their strengths or are used for research as their powers are vague and different from ours.

The Rulers have been sending servants to both the Human and Demonic realms to gather knowledge of their invasion plans, but these servants, although holy and extremely powerful, don’t always make it back to the Holy Realm. Both sides take these servants head on and prepare themselves when a visit is planned, most of the time to take their lives. The Rulers are outraged by the efforts of both races. 

In the last 10 years, as a reaction to murdering the servants, the Rulers have been making both of the lesser realms a living hell. Many “natural” disasters like droughts, tsunami’s and extreme thunderstorms have taken many lives from both sides. ## Small human lore dump
Humans are by far not an innocent race. They are greedy, selfish, have bad habits like gambling and destroy everything they touch. The higher gods, the weatherkin, are done with them. They plan to wipe off all humans of the earth to start fresh with a new type of human that is more loving towards themselves, the earth and the rulers; one that does not make war. Similarly they plan to get rid of most of the lesser deities, as they have been corrupted by the same sins as humanity. Within the human realm, not even those who have lived their lives without sin will be spared.

Incredible thunderstorms with heavy rain and immeasurable winds have been terrorising the human realm, slowly destroying villages and even mountains from the earth. Immediately following up with droughts and blizzards. As if the Rulers plan to take all life from earth. 

## Contract magic
Some demons have been making contracts with humans. Hierarchically, demons are by far the stronger race, but there is almost an infinite amount of humans to go up against. By making the humans stronger and putting restrictions on the use of these powers against the demons themselves, the demons are making humanity a bigger threat to the Rulers. But for humanity this is a risk worth taking, if it means a higher chance of survival. 

These deals most often have almost impossible odds for humans with unfair rules to bind them to the contract. Upon breaking a contract, people risk giving themselves a loved one to the demonic realm or to be made an underling of the demon. Most of the rules are unknown to current practitioners of these powers, as only the original signer knows what is written in the contract. These signers are most likely dead for hundreds of years. 

Those who have taken these powers have created temples, villages and monasteries for themselves to train themselves and others in these powers. Over a couple of hundred years, the world has changed from simple martial arts to many fighting styles that manipulate one or multiple elements with each punch or strike they give. Most people use at least a little bit of these powers in their day to day lives, even if they are not warriors. Humans have used these powers for so long that they see this as their own powers, not that of a demon.
## Recent attacks
A small village in the Bunshin Mountain range was completely decimated and wiped from the earth. The village was known for creating both poisons and remedies from the venom of snakes and other poisonous creatures. Only the elder of the city, an unnamed woman who has lived for over 170 years solely living on these unknown remedies, has survived the attack.

It was another servant from the Rulers. A lesser deity called Vedda, a servant with the body of a 5 meter tall (15ft.) human-esq peacock visited the village with unknown orders. The purpose of the visit is unknown, but it is rumoured that the village was “asked” to hand in their recipe for these poisons and cures and denied the offer.

Upon denial, the village stood against the wrath of the servant. They were prepared for death, as they were not willing to give their recipes to the invader. There was no other choice to make and as they waited silently for their dead an outsider intervened. 

A human with the powers of the Phoenix set foot in the village to destroy Vedda and protect the village. In the end, the villagers were killed by the invader, as they were simply in the way of the fight, but the human outsider did succeed in killing Vedda. He potentially saved other villagers from this fate. The outsider left the scene without a word, but he did leave the lifeless birds’ body behind after performing a short spell on it.

What remains unknown is that shortly after the attack is that the body of the phoenix disappeared but nobody can explain where it went or who it had taken.

## Current 
In the meantime, the disasters on earth have wiped at least 30% of the population. About half of the land has been destroyed or has been taken by the oceans, only some small inhabitable islands remain in the ocean. The mainland is becoming smaller by the year and some folks are often moving further inwards, thus making some enormous cities in the middle of the continent. 

The [[(7) Shogun]], the current leader of this part of the world, has announced plans to bring together a team of highly skilled warriors to investigate the plans of the Rulers. From within the nation heroic warriors with a certain title from each of the biggest monasteries or other influential households* will be brought together to be sent into the Seven Heavens, Realm of the Rulers. They will have to travel through the demonic realm in search for seven keys to open the Ruler’s Gates and traverse the holy lands within. Only a handful of people are sent on this mission every 25 years, but so far none have gathered all the keys. This new generation has the highest potential to finally succeed on this mission.

*temple, family, school, etc.* 
# Clarification
## Demons
Demons in this world are not simply like demons and devils. Most of them are humanoid hybrids between humans and animal-like creatures. They are not inherently evil, but they are hostile against humans, just like humans are hostile to them. There are demons who represent the demonic view from our world, but they are more rare and most often are known as devils.
