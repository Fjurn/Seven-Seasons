## Crumb of Courage (Pan Xul) 
---
a) Once per day, gain advantage on a:
- Fear save
- Moral dilemma
- Persuasion vs innocence (zoals children/spirits/etc)
b) Once, you may also use it to auto succeed on a death save, after that the boon is lost.
---
## Pocket Mirror of the Queen (reaction)
---
a) Once per long rest, you may “mirror” another creature’s attack roll (friend or foe):
- When you see a creature within 10ft of you make an attack roll, you copy that roll for your own attack. You must do so before you know the value and result of the roll. For your next attack, you must use the new roll as your own attack.
`It’s not advantage — it’s fey symmetry.`
