In my campaign I have swapped the usual skills and feats for custom Talent Trees. This is a homebrew ruleset made by ImFromNASA. The full document can be found here: [link](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1VvHtaIhUOam4VkNB_lSmn-olS_oQmIYU/view)


You can invest points in skills you are proficient with. Each time you would receive an ability score improvement (ACI) and would otherwise be able to choose a feat, you will now get 2 skill points. These points can be used on any skill proficiency you already have and invest in any tier to go to the next tier. 

You need 4 points to max a talent tree:
- (Base tier)
- (Either path A, B or C) -> A1, B1 or C1
- (The tree tier below A, B or C) -> A2, B2 or C2
- (Final tier)

For example: Acrobatics
- You have proficiency in **Acrobatics** and put 2 points into from your level up to 4.
	- You get **Handspring Expert** and then have to choose from path *A, B or C*. 
	- You choose to get **Tumble** (path *B*).
- On another level up where you get an ASI, you choose to get 2 points instead.
	- You go further down path *B* and get **Agility** and **Freerunning** to complete the Acrobatics Talent Tree.
