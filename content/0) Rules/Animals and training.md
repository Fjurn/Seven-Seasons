You can buy trained beasts. Based on the CR and size the price is determined by your DM. Additionally, the trained beasts abilities also add some value to the creature. 

DM decides based on a different table the base cost of the animal. Saddle prices for monsters replace the normal exotic saddles from PHB. 

Saddle prices per creature size

| Size       | Cost   | Weight |
| ---------- | ------ | ------ |
| Tiny       | 15gp   | 8lb    |
| Small      | 30gp   | 16lb   |
| Medium     | 60gp   | 40lb   |
| Large      | 180gp  | 80lb   |
| Huge       | 360gp  | 240lb  |
| Gargantuan | 1080gp | 720lb  |
Armor = 4* normal armor price


Training Cost
Roll 1d4-1 (minimum van 1) en dan 1d9 van onderstaande

Om een animal action te gebruiken moet je een animal handling check maken met een free action (wave, verbal, etc). Training a creature yourself can only be done per continuous downtime activity. Training usually costs 1 gold per day, meaning if you train a creature yourself it would cost 50 days to learn your companion the aid action. If you interrupt its training with more than 1 day, you may roll an animal handling check DC10+amount of days interrupted from your training. If you succeed, the interrupted day does not forgo your progress. But if you fail, your progress goes back 1d4+ days.
