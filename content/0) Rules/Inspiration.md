| Gaining  <br>inspiration  <br>(meditation) | Downtime activities | During downtime in Comfortable, Wealthy or Aristocratic environment, you gain enough rest through meditation or other means to gain more benefits. Roll a meditation check (nature/arcana/religion) based on the calmliness of the environment. When you succeed, you gain 1 inspiration. You can only attempt to gain inspiration this way once for the whole duration of downtime. You may meditate for a longer duration, but this will not affect the DC or points gained. You can have up to 2 inspiration. You must be consious to use or receive inspiration. | You can spend inspiration on yourself in the following ways:  <br>- Give yourself advantage on an attack roll, saving throw or ability check <br>- If you already had advantage, you may reroll one of the rolled dice instead.<br>- Reroll on a d100+ roll on a funny status effect  <br>- Increase critical range by 1 (costs 2 inspiration) | You can spend inspiration on others in the following ways, uses your reaction:  <br>- Roll 1 (or 2) hit dice on the spot  <br>- Add 1d4 on the next any attack roll, saving throw or ability check (within 1 minute, does not stack) |
