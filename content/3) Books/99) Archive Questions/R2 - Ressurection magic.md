- Question: What are the simplest or most accessible ways to obtain resurrection magic, or other forms of power, so that I, or a third party, could restore the pile of bones that was once Gertje to his living, intelligible state?

Antwoord:

only creatures in the Eternal Sea or higher possess resurrection magic. Creatures in Diyu exist who can also make items of that kind. 

edit: actually, Orcs would also be able to cast this type of magic, but they won't have enough internal magic force and therefor would either sacrifice another creature or themselves in exchange for the target of the spell. Thereby it is deemed as banned magic
