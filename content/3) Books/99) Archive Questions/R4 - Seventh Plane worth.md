
Concretely, what could the Seventh Plane possibly offer to each of the individual higher planes that would make the rulers of said planes willing to share their knowledge, skills, and power with us?


2a) The Seventh Plane (Cegeris) as a whole has not a lot of material goods to offer to higher planes.
- The Beastlands would likely comply with resources such as metals, ores, etc. You would have to yield 50% of the mines and forest to them and they would gain free roaming in your plane.
- The Wildlands would likely comply with humans to keep as pets or slaves.
- The Eternal Sea, Diyu and Euphoria would conquer Cegeris as their own, but humanity would cease to exist.

2b) Alternatively, as a growing presence and a group which has contacts and allies within parts of the realms, you could offer your services to each of the rulers or strong creatures. For example, some known problems in the realms that may come on your path:

- The Thunderbeast, ruler of the Beastlands, would be more friendly towards you if you return his son, the Prince of Lightning to him.
- The Commander of Horn and Gold, ruler of the Wildlands, wishes for the Queen of Night and Magic to perish. On the other hand, the Queen of Night and Magic wishes to overtake the Commander of Horn and Gold. She has the most loyal followers within the plane, but in comparison to the powers of the Commander they are far from able to touch him. Neither of them are easy targets, but you might win favor from either of them.
- In Diyu, there exists creatures who wish to exponentially grow in size and strength to transcend to Euphoria and are willing to do whatever it takes at a chance of Monarchism. One of these creatures goes by the name of the Lunarian. This creature has lost a part of their soul in each of the planes under Diyu and seeks to merge with them once more. Whenever it does so successfully and is completed, it is likely to ascend to Euphoria.
- Other creatures wihin the realms know of this wish of the Lunarian and forebid him from doing so. One of his opposers is the Queen of Night and Magic, as she is one of the three who imprisoned his brains in Cegeris.
- Sertrous, ruler of Diyu, would at his evil nature likely forbid the Lunarian from leaving Diyu. Helping him in destroying the Lunarian's wish would likely boost his view of you.
